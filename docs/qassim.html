      How it works

      1. Fields are mapped from ESA Sentinel-2 greenness: seasonal maximum NDVI from offset-corrected surface reflectance, 20 m.
      2. NISAR L-band radar (NASA-ISRO) gives three independent signals per field every 12 days: HV backscatter (crop canopy), 12-day coherence (surface being worked or irrigated) and soil moisture.
      3. A field is farmed in a season when its peak HV backscatter, measured above the same date's bare-desert median, beats a desert-patch tail threshold: median + 3 × (median − 16th percentile) of field-sized desert patches' seasonal peaks. The rule uses no Sentinel-2 input; Sentinel-2 is only the check.
      4. Water = farmed area × irrigation depth by class ÷ pivot efficiency; energy = water × pumping head ÷ pump efficiency; CO₂ for grid or diesel pumps.
      5. Wheat straw and palm fronds → biochar plant siting, with transport emissions for feed in and char out (back to the fields, or to the nearest operating cement plant). Removal is credited with the Puro.earth 2025 soil persistence model for both outlets.

      How this differs from the national run

      The national results map this ground too, in a separate run with fewer radar dates and a coarser grid, so the two are never added together.
